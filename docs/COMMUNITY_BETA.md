# Community beta test

Use this after the production health check passes and one real Essential order has completed end to end.

## Share links

- Product: `https://memoriestomelody.com`
- Guided feedback: `https://memoriestomelody.com/feedback`

## Suggested invitation

> I’m opening a small beta for Memories to Melody, a service that turns personal memories into a gift-ready song. Please try the full creation flow on your phone or computer and tell me where anything feels confusing, slow, or untrustworthy. Use the feedback link when you finish. Please do not include private information you would not want processed into a song.

## Test prompts

Ask testers to check:

1. Whether the package and price are clear before checkout.
2. Whether the story questions feel easy and respectful.
3. Whether generation progress changes without refreshing.
4. Whether the recipient gift email and private reveal are understandable.
5. Whether the listening page works on mobile, including audio, lyrics, download, and sharing.
6. Whether any language, accessibility, or trust issue would stop them from buying.

Review incoming submissions in **Admin → Support**. Tag the first ten by creation, checkout, progress, listening, accessibility, or other; fix repeated blockers before expanding the beta.
